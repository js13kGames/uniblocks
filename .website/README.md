Guide your unicorn companion safely home to the rainbow by stopping the rising wall of blocks! Swap adjacent tiles, steer falling blocks into position, and trigger high-scoring chain reactions across 10 escalating levels of puzzle-action.
### Feature Highlights
**Core Gameplay**
* **Save the Unicorn:** Keep the grid clean to help your unicorn stay happy—if the rising blocks overflow, the run is over!
* **Match & Clear:** Swap tiles to form lines of 3 or more matching colors. Land 4+ matches to trigger fast rainbow power-ups and boost your score.
* **Dynamic Falling Blocks:** Manage incoming falling blocks in real-time while continuously organizing the lower wall.
* **Bomb Mechanic:** Avoid prolonged delays without making matches, or dynamic bomb strikes will crack blocks and turn them into unswappable obstacles.
* **Special Rainbow Blocks:** Swap rainbow tiles to trigger screen-clearing cascades and destroy nearby cracked blocks.
**Game Features**
* **10 Progressive Levels:** Advance through increasingly tight tick timers, faster wall rises, and higher numbers of falling blocks—culminating in an intense finale at Level 10.
* **Cross-Platform Controls:** Built-in support for Keyboard, Mouse, and Mobile Touch inputs.
* **Save Progression:** Local storage tracks your highest scores and unlocked levels for each stage.

### Controls Overview
| Action | Keyboard | Mouse / Touch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Swap Blocks** | `Arrow Keys` + `Spacebar` | Click/Touch & Drag adjacent blocks
| **Shift Falling Block** | `A` / `D` keys | Click/Touch & Drag falling block sideways
| **Game Controls** | `P` (Pause), `R` (Restart), `M` (Menu) | In-game UI Buttons
